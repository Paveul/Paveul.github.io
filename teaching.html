Posets & Galois Connections

All around mathematics there are examples of partial orders. We study these structures and the basic tools used to analyze them, with extensions to lattices, poset dimension, ordinals, and Möbius inversion, culminating with Galois connections as a unifying framework for dualities.

Exotic Cousins of Integers

Ever got angry that there is no way to factorize x²+y²? Come to the dark side, where we treat i = √-1 as if it were an integer. This class introduces the algebraic language of rings needed to explore these exotic 'cousins' of integers and understand unique factorization.
