Posets & Galois Connections

Among the extension topics I included:

  • lattices – for the algebraically-inclined, my favourite example there being the lattice of partitions of a set,
  • dimension of posets – for the combinatorial-inclined; there are multiple difficult problems there;
  • ordinals & Axiom of Choice – for the logicians & set-theorists; here there is a little window into an important area of 'adult maths';
  • Möbius inversion – for the number-theorists; with a nice unification of the familiar inclusion-exclusion principle.
