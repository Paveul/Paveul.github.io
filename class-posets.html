Posets & Galois Connections

Maths Beyond Limits, Falenty

All around mathematics there are examples of partial orders—structures with a notion of an 'incomplete order', which determines some dominance but not necessarily on every pair of elements. In the first part of this class, we study these structures and the basic tools used to analyze them, with possible extensions to lattices, poset dimension, ordinals, and Möbius inversion.

The second part focuses on Galois connections—a unifying framework for certain dualities across mathematics, such as the correspondence between field extensions and automorphism groups in Galois theory, or between varieties and ideals in algebraic geometry.
