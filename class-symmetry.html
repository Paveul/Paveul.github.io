What is Symmetry, Actually?

Maths Beyond Limits, Falenty

The purpose of this class is gaining intuition for 'how symmetry works' and later structuring the understanding in terms of the very basic definitions of group theory. In fact, this can be regarded as an introduction to group theory via studying group actions. We aim to build intuition and give motivation for group actions, via 'playing' with concrete examples such as permutation puzzles, symmetric polynomials, and the Rubik's cube.
